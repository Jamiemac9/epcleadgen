ECO4 ends on 31 December 2026: what West Midlands homeowners can still do

ECO4, the scheme that pays for insulation and heating upgrades through energy suppliers, ends on 31 December 2026. The government has confirmed there will be no replacement supplier scheme, so if you are relying on ECO4 funding, the work needs to happen before the end of the year.

What the government decided

ECO4 was due to finish in March 2026. In January 2026 the government extended it by nine months, so it now ends on 31 December 2026. Energy suppliers' minimum targets for solid wall insulation and some other measures must also be met by that date.

The government also confirmed that there will be no successor supplier obligation to ECO4. Instead, it has committed £1.5 billion of extra grant funding for upgrading low-income households in fuel poverty, as part of the Warm Homes Plan.

If you are waiting on ECO4 work

  • Ask your installer for a firm installation date before 31 December 2026, in writing.
  • Check what happens if the work slips past the deadline. The government response sets no separate grace period for individual installs.
  • If you already had external wall insulation fitted under ECO4 or the Great British Insulation Scheme, the government is offering on-site audits, and installers are expected to put right any problems found.

Funding that is still open

The government response points households that can't get ECO4 towards two other routes:

  • Boiler Upgrade Scheme: a grant towards replacing a fossil fuel heating system with a heat pump or biomass boiler, in England and Wales.
  • Warm Homes: Local Grant: check your council's website for whether it is open in your area.

Eligibility and amounts differ for each, so check the official pages before you commit to any work.

What to do now

  1. If ECO4 work is booked, confirm the date and get it in writing.
  2. If not, run our eligibility check to see which routes might fit your home.
  3. Get an up-to-date EPC, so you know your current rating before choosing measures.
  4. Check the Boiler Upgrade Scheme and your council's Warm Homes: Local Grant pages for current eligibility.
