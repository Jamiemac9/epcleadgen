Government Grants & Local Authority Funding

The West Midlands Combined Authority retrofit funding pool works alongside national ECO4 obligations to deliver free and subsidised energy improvements to qualifying households.

01

ECO4 Scheme

The Energy Company Obligation phase 4 targets low-income and fuel-poor households. If your income is under £31,000 and your home has an EPC of D or below, you likely qualify.

02

Local Authority Flexible Eligibility (LA Flex)

Birmingham, Solihull and Coventry councils can nominate households that do not meet standard ECO4 criteria but still need support. This widens access to Birmingham boiler grants and insulation.

03

WMCA Retrofit Accelerator

The West Midlands Combined Authority retrofit funding accelerator provides additional top-up grants for complex properties and hard-to-treat homes across the region.
